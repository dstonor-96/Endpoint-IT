Privacy Policy

How Endpoint IT collects, uses, and protects your personal information.

Effective date: May 2026

Endpoint IT ("I", "me", "my") operates the website endpointit.com.au and provides IT support services in the Perth metropolitan area. This privacy policy explains how I collect, use, disclose, and protect your personal information in accordance with the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and the Australian Privacy Principles (APPs).

1. Information I Collect

I may collect the following personal information:

  • Name and contact details (email address, phone number, physical address)
  • Business name and ABN (for business clients)
  • Information about your IT systems necessary to provide support
  • Communications between us (emails, messages, call notes)
  • Payment and invoicing information

I collect this information directly from you when you enquire about services, engage me for work, or submit a form on this website.

2. How I Use Your Information

Your personal information is used to:

  • Provide and deliver IT support services you've requested
  • Communicate with you about your enquiry, booking, or ongoing service
  • Issue invoices and process payments
  • Maintain records of work performed for warranty and support continuity
  • Comply with legal obligations (tax records, insurance claims if applicable)

I do not use your information for marketing purposes without your explicit consent.

3. Sharing Your Information

I do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. I may share limited information with:

  • Service providers I use to operate the business (e.g., invoicing software, remote support tools, scheduling platforms) — only to the extent necessary to deliver services
  • Professional advisors (accountant, insurer) where required for business operations
  • Government bodies where required by law (e.g., ATO for tax compliance)

I take reasonable steps to ensure any third-party providers handle your information in accordance with the APPs.

4. Data Security

I take reasonable steps to protect your personal information from misuse, interference, loss, and unauthorised access. This includes:

  • Using encrypted password management (Bitwarden) for any stored credentials
  • Securing devices used for remote support
  • Limiting access to your information to only what's needed for service delivery

No method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure. While I strive to protect your information, I cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Accessing and Correcting Your Information

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information I hold about you
  • Request correction of any inaccurate or out-of-date information
  • Request deletion of your information (subject to legal retention requirements)

To make a request, contact me at contact@endpointit.com.au. I will respond within 30 days.

6. Cookies and Website Analytics

This website may use basic analytics to understand how visitors use the site (e.g., which pages are visited, general location data). No personally identifiable information is collected through analytics.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies will not affect the functionality of this website.

7. Changes to This Policy

I may update this privacy policy from time to time to reflect changes in my practices or legal requirements. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

8. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how I handle your personal information, please contact me:

  • Email: contact@endpointit.com.au
  • Business: Endpoint IT
  • Location: Perth, Western Australia

If you're not satisfied with my response, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC).
