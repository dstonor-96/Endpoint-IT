How Retainers Work

A retainer is a simple monthly arrangement: you pay a fixed fee, and you get a set amount of IT support hours, plus access to email or phone queries.

Most support is remote — I connect to your machine, sort the issue, and you get back to work. If something needs on-site attention, that's available on the Growth tier or at an agreed rate.

Every retainer is month-to-month. There's no lock-in period. If it stops making sense for your business, you cancel. No penalties, no exit fees, no awkward conversations.

Response time is next business day for all tiers. I'm upfront about that. If you need someone on-call 24/7, I'm not the right fit — and I'd rather tell you that now.

No Locked Contracts. Ever.

If a retainer stops making sense for your business, you cancel at the end of the month. No penalties, no exit clauses, no friction.
