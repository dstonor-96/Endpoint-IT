Services

What I Do

Straightforward IT services for homes and small businesses. No jargon, no upselling — just practical help that gets things working.

Residential Callouts

On-site IT support for your home. I come to you — evenings and weekends — and sort the problem in person.

What's Covered

  • New PC or laptop setup — unbox, configure, transfer your files
  • Virus and malware removal — clean your machine and secure it properly
  • Data transfer from old PC to new — documents, photos, bookmarks, the lot
  • Printer and peripheral setup — get everything connected and working
  • NBN troubleshooting — basic connectivity issues, router setup, Wi-Fi fixes
  • General help — software installs, email setup, hardware advice

Service area covers Perth's northern suburbs — Joondalup, Wanneroo, Clarkson, Butler, Currambine, and surrounds.

Small Business Retainers

Monthly IT support plans for small businesses. Remote-first, with on-site visits available on higher tiers.

What's Included

  • Remote support hours each month (varies by tier)
  • Email and phone queries
  • Microsoft 365 user management — adds, removes, password resets
  • Monthly backup health checks
  • Patch and update monitoring
  • Priority next-day response (Growth tier)
  • Monthly on-site visit (Growth tier)

All retainers are month-to-month. No locked contracts, no exit fees. Response time is next business day for all tiers.

M365 / Cloud Migration

Move your business email, files, and collaboration tools to Microsoft 365 — properly set up and documented.

What's Included

  • Email migration to Microsoft 365 (Exchange Online)
  • OneDrive setup for file storage and sharing
  • Microsoft Teams deployment
  • One training document covering day-to-day use
  • DNS and domain configuration

Priced per project based on number of users. Most migrations for small businesses are completed within a week.

Web Design (Add-On)

Simple, professional WordPress websites for existing clients. Not a web agency — just clean sites that work.

What's Included

  • 5-page WordPress website — Home, About, Services, Contact, plus one custom page
  • Mobile-responsive design
  • Basic SEO setup
  • Contact form integration
  • Optional ongoing hosting and updates

Web design is available as an add-on for existing retainer or callout clients. Best pricing is bundled with an active retainer.
