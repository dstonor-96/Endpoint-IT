About Endpoint IT

A sole-operator IT business built on practical help, honest pricing, and actually answering the phone.

The Short Version

I started Endpoint IT because I kept seeing the same problem — people in Perth's northern suburbs either paying too much for basic IT help, or not being able to find anyone who'd show up in the first place.

This is a one-person operation. I do the work myself. That means you get someone who actually knows your setup, remembers what was done last time, and doesn't pass you off to a different technician every visit.

I work evenings and weekends because that's when most people actually need help. Response time is next business day — I don't promise emergency turnarounds, and I'm upfront about that.

How I Work

No mission statement. No corporate values poster. Just a few things I stick to.

Insured — Always

Professional indemnity and public liability insurance are in place before any paid work happens. Non-negotiable.

Transparent Pricing

Every price is on the website. You know what you're paying before I start. No invoice surprises, no "well, actually" moments.

No Locked Contracts

Retainer plans are month-to-month. If it's not working for you, you stop. No penalties, no awkward phone calls.

Honest About Limits

I'm one person working evenings and weekends. I don't promise 1-hour SLAs or 24/7 emergency callouts. If that's what you need, I'll tell you so you can find the right fit.

Remote-First

Most problems get fixed remotely — faster for you, more efficient for everyone. On-site visits when they're actually needed, not as a default.

Practical, Not Perfect

I recommend what works and what you can afford. Not the fanciest solution — the right one.

Service Area

Endpoint IT is based in Perth's northern suburbs and primarily serves the Joondalup, Wanneroo, Clarkson, Butler, Currambine, and surrounding areas.

Remote support is available anywhere in Australia. On-site callouts outside the northern suburbs may incur a travel surcharge.
